CISM

Certified International Supply Chain Manager (CISM)

Lead global supply chains with confidence.

Training duration12 training hours / 15 learning hours
DeliveryFace-to-face or virtual instructor-led
ProgrammeProfessional certification
Course summary

Build expertise that creates measurable value.

In today's global business environment, organisations depend on skilled supply chain managers to optimise operations, strengthen supplier relationships, manage logistics, and drive sustainable business growth. Effective supply chain management plays a critical role in improving operational efficiency, reducing costs, and maintaining a competitive advantage across international markets. The Certified International Supply Manager (CISM) programme is designed to provide participants with advanced knowledge and practical skills in strategic supply chain management. The course equips professionals with the expertise to manage global supply chain operations, supplier management, logistics, inventory optimisation, sustainability, collaboration, and emerging technologies while developing strategic decision-making capabilities. Through practical learning and industry- focused frameworks, participants will develop the competencies required to manage complex supply chain networks, improve organisational performance, and create value in today's competitive global marketplace.
